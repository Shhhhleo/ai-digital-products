# Product formats (anything except digital minds)

Pick the format that delivers the transformation best, not the easiest one. A plain e-book on its own is never the default: if the best fit is an e-book, it ships with at least one companion (a tracker, template, checklist pack or a short video) so the buyer gets something to use, not just read.

## Choosing
- The fix is knowledge or a plan → e-book bundle (or a short programme if the audience prefers video).
- The fix is a daily habit, a schedule or tracking → a tool (planner, Notion, spreadsheet).
- The fix is physical or visual (movement, technique, cooking, training a pet, a skill) → pre-recorded programme.
- The fix is listening or practising (sleep, calm, language, confidence) → audio programme.
- The audience needs accountability → challenge kit.
- The creator is often on camera and trusted → presenter-led programme, using their own recordings or, with written consent, their trained likeness and voice.
- Tie-break: the format that can launch soonest without losing the transformation (Iman: start simple, add complexity once it sells).
