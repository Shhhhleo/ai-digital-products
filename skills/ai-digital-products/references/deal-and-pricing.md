# Deals, pricing and the partnership agreement (for Benji)

## Formats to offer
Any format in references/formats.md except digital minds, recommending one for the audience. A plain e-book alone is never offered; an e-book always comes with a companion asset. Start as simple as the transformation allows, and suggest a bigger bundle once the first one sells.

## Why 50/50 (how Benji explains it)
- They bring the audience and the trust. We bring everything else: 150+ source research, writing, design, video or template build, sales website, hosting, checkout, delivery, buyer support, updates.
- Zero cost and zero risk to them. No upfront fee, no time beyond approving the outline and sharing it.
- Both sides only earn when it sells, so both are pushing for the same thing.
- Payment is automatic: Whop splits every sale after its fees and drops their half into their Whop balance. They never have to chase us for money and can see every sale.
- It is ongoing: they earn on every sale for as long as the product sells, including months later.
- It is the standard split for this kind of partnership.

## Objections
- "I want more than 50%." Explain the work split and that we carry all the build cost. Hold 50/50. If they insist, Benji asks the user before offering anything else; never above 60% to the creator, and only for a creator whose views and engagement are clearly above the filters.
- "Can you pay me upfront?" No upfront fees; the revenue share is the payment. That keeps it zero-risk for both.
- "I could make it myself." True, and plenty mean to. We do it in weeks with 150+ sources and run the whole store. They can still put their name and method on it.
- "Will it hurt my audience's trust?" They approve the outline and the final product, nothing goes out without their yes, and there is a refund policy.
- "Who owns it?" Per the agreement below: co-owned product, the creator keeps their name and content; either side can end it with notice.
- "Is this a scam?" Offer a short call with the user, share a live Reset Lab product link as a sample, and point out they never pay anything.

## Pricing (agree a band with the creator; Advaith approves the number)
Price rises with complexity, using the bands in references/formats.md: simple $19-37, tools and audio $37-67, programmes and challenge kits $77-150, bundles $97-150. Price on the transformation and the audience's purchasing power; Iman's partnership example treats $150 as the low end, which only fits programmes and bundles for audiences that clearly spend. Launch week at the bottom of the band, then the standard price. Never below $19. Honest launch pricing only.

Expectations to share honestly with the creator if asked: a launch typically converts a small fraction of a percent to about 1% of followers. Example: 50,000 followers x 0.5% x $27 ≈ $675 in launch sales, split 50/50. No guarantees.

## Agreement outline (agreement.md, one page; the user reviews and signs)
1. Parties: Advaith / Reset Lab and the creator (name, handle, email).
2. Product: title, format, promise, price, launch date.
3. Who does what: Reset Lab builds, hosts, sells, delivers, supports; creator supplies input, approves outline and final product, promotes (agreed posts for launch week, then at least one mention a month while live).
4. Money: 50/50 of net revenue after Whop fees, paid automatically via Whop's revenue share; refunds and chargebacks reduce both shares the same way; each side handles its own taxes.
5. Ownership: the product is jointly owned for as long as the partnership runs; the creator keeps rights to their name, likeness and pre-existing content; Reset Lab keeps its research, templates and site code.
6. Approval: nothing launches or changes materially without both sides' yes.
7. Term and exit: ongoing; either side can end with 30 days' notice; on exit, sales stop or the product is rebranded, as agreed then.
8. Conduct: no misleading claims; health or money products carry the disclaimer.
9. Signatures and date.
This is a plain-language template, not legal advice. For larger deals the user should get it checked.
